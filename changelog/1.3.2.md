# Glimt 1.3.2 (09/10/2026)

Micro reminders get more personal: pick the breathing pattern they use, and put them wherever suits you on screen.

## New features

  - Choose which breathing preset your micro reminders use
  - Micro reminders now stay where you drag them, even after a restart

## Improvements

  - A fresh new default font throughout the app
  - Main breathing window and Settings window are now a touch more translucent
  - Tidier preset management, with a clearer confirmation when deleting a preset
