# Glimt 1.3.0 (02/10/2026)

See your practice take shape. Glimt now keeps track of your breathing: detailed statistics, daily streaks you can set to your own rhythm, and a calmer, reorganised Settings window.

## New features

  - **Breath statistics**: a new Statistics page shows your sessions, breathing time, breaths taken, days practiced and more. It also covers the anatomy of your breath, like your inhale-to-exhale ratio, breaths per minute and time spent in each phase.
  - **Streaks**: build a daily breathing habit with a streak badge and a weekly view. Today's progress shows as a small ring that fills as you go.
  - **Streak settings**: you decide what counts as a streak day (a minimum number of sessions or a completion rate) and how rest days are handled. If streaks feel more stressful than helpful, you can turn them off completely.

## Improvements

  - The Settings window is now grouped into clear sections (Breathing, Statistics, Schedule, Playlists, Focus, Automation, System), and new features are highlighted so they're easy to find.
  - Stats and streaks follow your own local calendar day, wherever you are in the world.
  - Visual polish across settings, presets, playlists and the breathing animation.

## Bug Fixes

  - Fixed the weekly streak badge putting days in the wrong column for users west of UTC.
  - Fixed small rounding issues in a few settings values.
  - Fixed an issue where a license could stop being recognised on the same machine after its network name changed. Glimt now re-links your license automatically.
