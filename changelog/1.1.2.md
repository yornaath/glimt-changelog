# Glimt 1.1.2 (07/06/2026)

Glimt is now on Windows! 🎉 Plus a gentle warm-up for your playlists.

## New

  - **Glimt comes to Windows** 🎉 — after living on macOS, Glimt now has a second home. Everything is here: guided breathing, reminders that arrive when you're actually at your desk, custom presets and playlists — dressed in native Windows window controls so it feels at home from the first breath. Welcome, Windows folks. We're glad you're here.

## Improvements

  - **Get-ready countdown for playlists** — playlists now ease you in with a short countdown before the first section begins, giving you a moment to settle and prepare before you start breathing.
