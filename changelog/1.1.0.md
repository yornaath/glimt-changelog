# Glimt 1.1.0 (05/06/2026)

Your breath, your way. This release brings custom breathing presets and playlists — so you can craft your own patterns and string them together into guided sequences.

## New Features

  - **Custom breathing presets** — create, edit, and delete your own breathing patterns and pick them anywhere in the app.
  - **Playlists** — combine multiple presets into a single guided sequence, with full editing and reordering of sections.

## Improvements

  - Refreshed onboarding flow to get you breathing sooner.
  - Redesigned settings with clearer cards for presets, playlists, and prompts.
  - Smoother, more fluid breathing orb animations.
  - More reliable system tray behavior.

## Bug Fixes

  - Fixed playlist creating, editing, and deleting so changes save reliably.
  - Fixed a form state issue where toggles wouldn't reset correctly when reopened.
