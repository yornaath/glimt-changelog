# Glimt 1.1.1 (05/06/2026)

A small polish release that makes playlists easier to follow at a glance, plus a fix for first-time setup.

## Improvements

  - **Playlist progress panel** — while a playlist plays, you now see its name and each section laid out, with a live progress bar tracking exactly where you are. Tap any section to jump straight to it.

## Bug Fixes

  - Fixed first launch on fresh installs where the app could fail to create its data storage.
