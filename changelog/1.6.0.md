# Glimt 1.6.0 (09/11/2026)

Pause a session whenever you need to, pick sessions from picture cards, choose light or dark, and let Glimt step aside while you game.

## New feature

  - Pause and resume a breathing session with the play/pause button or the Space bar
  - Smart pause for gaming: reminders wait while you play (Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox and fullscreen games)
  - Separate switches for pausing during meetings and during games
  - Choose a light, dark or system theme in Settings
  - Presets and playlists are shown as picture cards in onboarding and the session pickers
  - Breath phases can be set in tenths of a second

## Improvements

  - A softly moving gradient background behind your breathing visuals
  - Session controls fade away while you're not moving the mouse
  - Smoother breathing animations
  - The tray menu shows why reminders are auto-paused, without the empty line
  - Light mode polish
  - AI agents get clearer MCP tool descriptions and can change single settings
  - Custom visualization errors clear once the visualization runs fine again

## Bug Fixes

 - Fixed microphone and camera detection on recent Windows 11 updates, so reminders pause during calls again
 - Licensed users no longer briefly see the paywall at startup
