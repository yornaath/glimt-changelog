# Glimt 1.6.1 (14/11/2026)

A fresh new look for Glimt, and one place to set your get-ready countdown.

## New feature

  - New app icon, logo and menu bar icons
  - Set the get-ready countdown for playlists too, next to the session length, so presets and playlists share one setting

## Improvements

  - Breath phases and countdowns now stay in step more precisely
  - Clearer playlist progress bar and session screen polish
  - Dark is now the default theme for new installs
  - Smoother background animation

## Bug Fixes

 - Fixed the countdown seconds sometimes being shown differently in different places
